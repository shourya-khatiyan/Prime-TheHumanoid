what do you think training or finetuning a model means? why is there even need to finetune a model when it is already pretrained on much larger dataset??
understand it with this exampe:
think of a college student pursuing btech computer science, he has vast knowledge of his domain, he is trained on a general skill set and that is problem solving (DSA). during his training he is given DSA problems and he solve the increasing his problem solving capabilities.
and one day a company comes and hire him for a python developer role???
but he's not industrial knowledge in python? then why did company chose him.
they chose him because he have a general skill set called "Problem Solving", after they onboard him, they will train him (finetuning) for his specific role. they will give him tasks, they will guide him and finally when he learns everything for that specific tasks, he is officially a python developer.

this is same with the general models, they are trained on a general data. even the humanoid specific models like GROOT N1, we need to finetune it so that it can adopt our humaniod's embodiment
