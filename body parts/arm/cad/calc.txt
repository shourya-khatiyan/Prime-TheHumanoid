=== 4 stops and falls ===========================================
unpowered fall horizontal -> straight: 0.62 J, 8.7 rad/s (2.6 m/s at the hand); ODESC idle = open phases, no braking (shorted phases would give only 0.067 N*m*s/rad)
  extension stop (heel tab): sleeve at r 64 mm; absorbing 0.62 J in 1 mm -> average 623 N (312 N per plate edge)
  extension stop (heel tab): sleeve at r 64 mm; absorbing 0.62 J in 3 mm -> average 208 N (104 N per plate edge)
  flexion stop (front edge): sleeve at r 83 mm; absorbing 0.62 J in 1 mm -> average 623 N (312 N per plate edge)
  flexion stop (front edge): sleeve at r 83 mm; absorbing 0.62 J in 3 mm -> average 208 N (104 N per plate edge)
driven into a stop at the 180 deg/s cap: 0.081 J kinetic + current limit 1.84 N*m -> 22 N static at the flexion sleeve
result: a powered stall is harmless; for an unpowered fall the 3 mm rubber pad on the heel face (part 218) gives the 3 mm travel (~200 N); keep the arm hanging (0 deg) whenever the ODESC is idle
