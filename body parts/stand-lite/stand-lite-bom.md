# Stand-lite — cover for the wooden stand

A thin frame under the existing wood plate, and a thin triangular sleeve on the existing stick. The wood already carries the arm. These prints are only the cover.

The heavy stand in `body parts/stand` is unchanged.

## Print

Bambu P2S, PETG or PLA. Longest piece 208 mm. The bed allows 246 mm.

`cad/stand_lite_print.zip` has one STL for each piece you print. Eight files.

| File | Qty | Grams, solid bars |
|---|---|---|
| base_a | 1 | 66 |
| base_b | 1 | 65 |
| stick_front_1, _2, _3 | 3 | 10 each |
| stick_back_1, _2, _3 | 3 | 10 each |
| **All plastic** | **8** | **about 194 g** |

The grams are the model volume at 1.27 g/cm³. The bars are thin, so print them solid. There is no infill to add.

## Already on the stand

| Item | Size |
|---|---|
| Wood plate | 340 × 190 mm. The frame's opening is 1.6 mm larger, so the plate drops in. |
| Wood stick | 27 × 18 mm, about 620 mm long. The sleeve clips around it. |

## Fit

1. Push the tongue of `base_a` into the slot of `base_b`. Two M3 screws.
2. Drop the wood plate into the frame. Four screws up into the wood.
3. For each stick segment, put the front and back together. The two pins locate them. Two M3 screws close the ears. Slide the three segments onto the stick, leaving the ends bare where the stick meets the plate and the shoulder.
