# Shoulder v6 — bought parts

Screws, bearings, and pins for one actuator. The motor and the encoder are not on this list. Printed parts are in `cad/actuator_print.zip`.

The journals are Ø21. The roller bore is 20 mm, and a Ø20 journal was still loose.

## Bearings

| Qty | Part | Size | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Needle roller and cage, no inner ring. SKF K 20×24×10 | 20 × 24 × 10 mm | One on each crank journal. Static rating 12.9 kN. The cage is one piece, so the rear one goes on before the flange. |
| 2 | Deep groove ball bearing. SKF 61906 | 30 × 47 × 9 mm | Rear hub, then front hub. The printed seat is Ø47.6. Shaft shoulder is Ø33. Housing shoulder is Ø44. |

## Pins

The eight Ø6 output pins are part of the rear carrier. They are not bought separately.

| Qty | Part | Size | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 46 | Ring pin, hardened steel, slip fit | Ø4 × 41 mm | Through the cycloid. Rear end in the housing, front end in the cover. Printed holes are Ø4.6. |
| 1 | Cross pin, hardened steel, slip fit | Ø4 × 36 mm | Through the arm flange and the hub. Holes are Ø4.1. |

## Screws

| Qty | Part | Size | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Socket head cap screw, ISO 4762 | M2 × 8 | Crank flange to the rotor face. Circle diameter 38 mm. The head pocket is 2.25 mm deep, so the 2 mm head stays inside the flange. |
| 3 | Countersunk screw, ISO 10642 | M3 × 8 | Crank flange to the crank shaft. Circle diameter 9 mm. Heads face the rotor. |
| 8 | Countersunk screw, ISO 10642 | M3 × 6 | Front plate to the output pin ends. |
| 4 | Countersunk screw, ISO 10642 | M3 × 8 | Bearing retainer to the housing. |
| 1 | Socket head cap screw, ISO 4762 | M4 × 12 | Front washer to the crank nose. Holds the front needle cage. |
| 8 | Socket head cap screw, ISO 4762 | M3 × 25 | Rear plate to the housing. Circle diameter 99 mm. |
| 8 | Socket head cap screw, ISO 4762 | M3 × 12 | Front cover to the housing. Same circle. |
| 6 | Socket head cap screw, ISO 4762 | M5, length set by the arm | Arm to the flange. Circle diameter 52 mm. |

ISO 10642 length includes the head. Use one grade for the steel screws, 12.9. The M2 screws go into the rotor, so use the torque the motor maker allows.

## Filament

PETG for the fit-check print. Solid mass is 698 g, of which the housing is 211 g and the cover is 92 g. A sparse housing is an estimate, not the solid mass.
